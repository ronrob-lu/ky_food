# 🍽️ KY Food Mod for Minetest & Luanti 🍔🍕

Welcome to **KY Food**, the ultimate culinary and kitchen expansion mod for **Minetest** and **Luanti**! Featuring **200 ultra-detailed 3D models** created by the world-famous asset artist [Kenney.nl](https://www.kenney.nl), **KY Food** brings an unprecedented level of flavor, ambiance, and delight to your voxel adventures.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🍕 **200 Stunning 3D Items**: From mouth-watering burgers, sizzling pizzas, and gourmet sushi to fresh produce, decadent desserts, and stylish kitchen utensils!
- 🍎 **Dual Engine Compatibility**: Seamlessly works out-of-the-box with both **Minetest Game (`default`)** and **MineClone2 / VoxeLibre (`mcl`)**!
- 🥤 **Full Healing & Nutrition System**: Every edible dish and beverage restores HP/hunger, keeping you energised and ready for adventure.
- 👨‍🍳 **Crafting & Creative Ready**:
  - Craft delicious dishes and drinks when ingredients are available!
  - All beverages are craftable using milk or water buckets/containers.
  - Natural foods (coconuts, wild fruits, seafood) remain uncraftable rewards for gatherers and explorers.
  - Retrieve any item instantly from the Creative Inventory!
- 🎨 **Unified Vibrant Palette**: Powered by `food_colormap.png` for clean, lightweight performance with high-definition visual appeal.

## 📜 How to Obtain Items

### 1. Creative Mode
Open your inventory in Creative Mode to browse through all 200 items in the `ky_food` collection!

### 2. Survival Crafting
If the necessary ingredients are present in your game environment:
- **Drinks & Beverages**: Craftable using water or milk combined with fruit/coffee/tea ingredients.
- **Cooked Dishes & Bakery**: Craftable with flour/wheat, sugar, meat, or vegetables using standard recipe patterns.
- **Natural / Uncraftable Items**: Unique ingredients like raw fish, coconuts, and wild berries are gatherable from nature!

---

## 🛠️ Requirements & Installation

1. Download or clone this repository into your Minetest `mods/` directory as `ky_food`.
2. Enable `ky_food` in your world settings.
3. Compatible with Minetest / Luanti 5.0.0+.
